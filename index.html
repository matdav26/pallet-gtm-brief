          Definitions

          • Direct competitors sell AI agents into logistics (brokers, carriers, shippers), the same buyers as Pallet: HappyRobot, Augment, Vooma, Raft.
          • Series C+ peers are enterprise AI agent companies at Series C or later that already sell abroad: HappyRobot (logistics), Sierra (customer service) and Taktile (financial services). They show how a motion like Pallet's scales internationally, not who Pallet loses deals to.
          • Entry-level hires are SDR/BDR roles and titles marked junior, associate or entry-level. Every other GTM role (AEs, sales engineers, managers, leaders, growth) counts as senior.
            • \g<1>
          • Readiness counts which of four role types (seller, SDR, sales engineer, local leader) Pallet has posted in a market: the roles companies ahead of Pallet build abroad.

          Limits of the data

          • Postings show hiring intent, not hires: a role may be unfilled, paused, or filled by referral without a posting.
          • Counts include open and past postings, and time windows differ by company (Raft's data starts in 2023, Augment's in 2026).
          • Ashby postings carry no date. Duplicates across boards, reposts and retitles are merged, which may occasionally merge two real openings.
            • \g<1>\n
          • Pallet\'s new markets (Dubai, Seoul, Tokyo) come from company plans, not from postings.

          Source

          • CrustData job index (LinkedIn, Ashby, YC Work at a Startup) and company records, pulled 28–29 Sep 2026.
          • Sales & GTM roles only; marketing, recruiting and revenue operations roles excluded.
